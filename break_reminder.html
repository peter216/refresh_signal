Body Reset · Even Hours
Your checklist
  • 01
    Stand up and move
    Walk to another room, step outside if you can — even 2 minutes of light movement resets blood pressure and leg circulation.
  • 02
    Box breathing
    Inhale 4 sec → hold 4 sec → exhale 4 sec → hold 4 sec. Repeat 4×. Activates the parasympathetic nervous system.
  • 03
    Stretch it out
    Neck rolls, shoulder shrugs, seated spinal twist, wrist circles. Target whatever held tension from the last hour.
  • 04
    Drink water
    A few ounces minimum. Refill if you're running low. Mild dehydration is a silent productivity killer.
Yellow
Mind Reset · Odd Hours
Your checklist
  • 01
    Eyes off the screen
    Close your eyes for 60 seconds or look at something 20+ feet away. Lets your ciliary muscles fully relax.
  • 02
    Brain dump
    Grab paper or a scratch file and offload whatever's bouncing around in your head. Clears working memory and reduces cognitive load for the next block.
  • 03
    Deliberate non-thinking
    Step away and let your mind wander for 3–5 minutes — no podcasts, no phone. Your default mode network does its best synthesis work here.
  • 04
    Intention reset
    What's the single most important thing for the next hour? Name it explicitly before you sit back down. Prevents drift back into reactive mode.
